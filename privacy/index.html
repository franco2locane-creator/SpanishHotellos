Privacy Policy

Spanish4Hoteleros (iOS app) · Effective 2 October 2026

In short
  • We store your account, study settings and practice results so the app works and you can see your progress.
  • For AI exercises we send the text of what you said, plus the exercise text, to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) to generate the guest's replies and your feedback. Never your audio, name or email. Only after you agree in the app.
  • Apple turns your speech into text (Apple's speech recognition). We never record or store audio.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps, no analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account and all its data in the app at any time: Settings › Delete account.

1. Who we are

Spanish4Hoteleros is published by Franco Lo Cane, the Netherlands ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used (we are the "controller" under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). Contact: Franco2.locane@gmail.com.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

The app asks for microphone and speech-recognition access so you can answer out loud. Your speech is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition, which may process the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app does not record, keep or send audio to us or to Anthropic. The guest's voice is generated on your device.

4. AI processing by Anthropic

AI role-plays, mock exams and the placement test use Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, PBC (USA). Before your first AI exercise the app asks for your explicit agreement; without it, no data is sent to Anthropic. When you agree and do an AI exercise, our server sends Anthropic:

  • the transcribed text of your answers and the conversation so far;
  • the exercise text (the scenario, the guest's role and the exercise goals).

We do not send your name, email address, account identifier or audio. Anthropic processes this data on our behalf to generate the guest's replies and your feedback. Under Anthropic's commercial terms it does not use this data to train its models. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings; AI exercises are then unavailable, while vocabulary, grammar and drills keep working.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account (Settings › Delete account), your account and all data linked to it are deleted from our database straight away, and the app's local data is removed from your device. Our providers may keep backups or logs for a limited period under their own policies. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records as their legal obligations require. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, contact Franco2.locane@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted per user on our database. The keys to our AI provider and database administration stay on our servers and are never part of the app.

9. Children

The app is made for hotel school students and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and if the change is significant we will tell you in the app. If we change what is sent for AI processing, the app will ask for your agreement again.
