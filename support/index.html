Spanish4Hoteleros — speaking practice for hotel school Spanish oral exams (iPhone)

Contact
Questions, problems or feedback: Franco2.locane@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and what happened.

Signing in

New accounts are created with Sign in with Apple. If you have an older account with an email address and password, choose "Sign in" and use those. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account.

AI practice units

AI role-plays and mock exams use AI practice units: one role-play uses 1 unit, a mock exam uses 2. Your purchase includes 10 units in total. The subscription adds 60 units every calendar month (they reset on the 1st). If the AI service fails before its first reply, the unit is given back. Vocabulary, grammar and drills never use units.

Subscription

Premium Monthly costs €7.99 per month (or the local equivalent) and renews automatically until you cancel. To cancel or change it: in the app, Settings › Subscription › Manage subscription, or on your iPhone, Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. If you reinstall the app or use a new device, tap "Restore subscription" on the subscription screen.

"AI practice is temporarily unavailable"

The AI service can't be reached right now. Please try again later — the unit for that exercise is not used. Vocabulary, grammar and drills keep working.

The microphone or speech recognition doesn't work

On your iPhone, open Settings › Spanish4Hoteleros and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. The app uses Apple's speech recognition and never records or stores audio.

AI processing

Before your first AI exercise the app asks for your agreement to send the transcribed text of your answers and the exercise text to Anthropic, the AI provider. You can withdraw it anytime in the app under Settings › AI processing.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings › Delete account. This deletes your account and all its data. It does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel that in your iPhone settings as described above.
